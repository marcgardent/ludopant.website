The modules

A module is a .bn file of node groups. Once the pack is installed, a graph calls it with use, and Blender builds its groups.

ModuleWhat it gives
biomesBiomes of several scatter layers in one graph (trees, bushes, rocks and grass from collections), each with its own density, spacing, slope and altitude rules, kept its radius off the layers before it; level of detail by distance to the camera with a proxy, thinning and culling far away, and view-frustum culling from a camera's own projection or a field of view
scatterRule-based scattering on a surface: points by density per square metre with Poisson-disk spacing, weighted by a named attribute or vertex group, kept where the rules allow (slope range, altitude band, clearance from paths, curves or points, noise patches), their mask soft at its edges; instances aligned to the normal or up, spun, tilted and scaled at random for a seed, smaller at the edge of the mask; a later layer kept a radius off an earlier one

Free in Base Node, and what the pack adds

Free and open source, on the Base Node site: the language, the Blender add-on, the formatter and parsers, and the free modules of the standard library. The pack adds the modules above, their showcase files and this tutorial.
