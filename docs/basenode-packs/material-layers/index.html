A layer carries its PBR channels together (base color, metallic, roughness, normal, height, emission, coat); layers stack by mask, by height where the higher one wins, or by blend modes per channel, with a detail layer and normals combined by reoriented or whiteout normal mapping, into a Principled BSDF. Triplanar and box projection of any texture, triplanar normal maps, and detail coordinates tiled at another scale.

The modules

A module is a .bn file of node groups. Once the pack is installed, a graph calls it with use, and Blender builds its groups.

ModuleWhat it gives
material_layersLayered materials in shader nodes: a layer is a Bundle of PBR channels (base color, metallic, roughness, normal, height, emission, coat), stacked by mask, by height (the higher wins, with a contrast) or by blend modes per channel (multiply, screen, overlay), a detail layer, normals combined by reoriented or whiteout normal mapping, and a Principled BSDF of the result
projectionsTextures without UVs for layered materials: triplanar projection (sharpness, scale per axis, object or world space) and box mapping of a texture given as a Closure, triplanar normal maps blended by whiteout per axis, and the coordinates of a detail texture tiled at another scale

Free in Base Node, and what the pack adds

Free and open source, on the Base Node site: the language, the Blender add-on, the formatter and parsers, and the free modules of the standard library. The pack's modules build on the free module math. The pack adds the modules above, their showcase files and this tutorial.
