Faces inset by a distance and extruded along their normals in divisions, twisted, tapered and random a face, one by one or as regions; edges extruded along a vector. Every edge of a closed mesh bevelled, flat or rounded on a circular profile in segments, and corners chamfered off the selected points. Holes filled, surfaces given thickness, booleans that mark the faces the cutter made and their seam, and grooved panel lines. Each tool stores what it made (front, sides, rows, strips, corners, rims, cuts) as attributes the next one selects.

The modules

A module is a .bn file of node groups. Once the pack is installed, a graph calls it with use, and Blender builds its groups.

ModuleWhat it gives
modelingModelling helpers that keep their groups: a surface given thickness with its outer, inner and rim faces marked (solid_outer, solid_inner, solid_rim), a boolean of a mesh and a cutter that marks the faces the cutter made (bool_cut) and the seam where they meet the mesh (bool_seam), and grooved panel lines along a set of edges (panel_groove)
poly_bevelBevels Geometry Nodes has no node for: every edge between two faces chamfered or rounded, an open mesh kept open, its segments laid on a profile from flat to square, each face inset, a strip an edge and a patch a corner (bevel_face, bevel_edge, bevel_corner); corners cut off the selected points of a mesh (chamfer_corner); the holes of a mesh filled, an n-gon or a fan of triangles a hole (fill_face)
poly_extrudeExtrusions as a modelling package makes them: faces inset by a distance (exact on planar faces), pushed out along their normals in divisions, each step turned and tapered about the middle of its front, one by one or as regions, their distance random a face for a seed; edges pushed out along a vector; poly_front (faces), poly_front_edge (edges) and poly_side mark what they made, poly_layer the row of each side, the marks of earlier tools kept

Free in Base Node, and what the pack adds

Free and open source, on the Base Node site: the language, the Blender add-on, the formatter and parsers, and the free modules of the standard library. The pack adds the modules above, their showcase files and this tutorial.
