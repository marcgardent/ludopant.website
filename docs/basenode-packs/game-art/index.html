Flowmaps derived from slope, drawn along curves and turned around obstacles, stored as the color attribute engines read in the frame of the UV map, with the two-phase shader that pans a texture by them; trim sheets that map the strips of a mesh to the row their width calls for, and strips along curves; roads along curves with lanes, shoulders and banking, laid on a terrain, mapped in metres, with a mask for blending and clearing scatter and the ground flattened under them; lightmap UVs packed without overlap, vertex colors baked for engines, and level of detail by distance.

The modules

A module is a .bn file of node groups. Once the pack is installed, a graph calls it with use, and Blender builds its groups.

ModuleWhat it gives
flowmapsFlowmaps for game engines: a flow field on a mesh derived from its slope, drawn along curves, deflected around obstacles, smoothed, and read back from a painted flowmap; stored as a color attribute in the UV frame of each face corner, RG 0 to 1 with 0.5 at rest and B the strength; and the shader side that pans a texture by the flowmap with the two-phase blend
game_exportMeshes ready for a game engine: a second UV map for lightmaps, unwrapped at sharp edges and packed into the unit square without overlap; fields or mesh maps baked into a vertex color; and level of detail by distance, the mesh collapsed by vertex clustering in cells that double with each step away from a viewer
roadsRoads for game levels: a road mesh along each spline of a curve, its carriageway of lanes between shoulders that drop away, banked by an angle and into its turns, laid on a terrain mesh along its centre or at every point, smoothed along its length, mapped across its width and along its length in metres; its mask, lanes and distances stored for blending and for clearing scatter; and the terrain under it masked or flattened to it
trim_sheetsTrim sheets for game art: the rows of an atlas, from the top, each a ratio of the one above; the faces or strips of a mesh mapped to the row their width calls for at the sheet's texel density, along their long side, with a random offset and mirror per strip or per face; and strips along curves, flat or upright, mapped along their length; UVs on the face corners and the row on the faces

Free in Base Node, and what the pack adds

Free and open source, on the Base Node site: the language, the Blender add-on, the formatter and parsers, and the free modules of the standard library. The pack adds the modules above, their showcase files and this tutorial.
