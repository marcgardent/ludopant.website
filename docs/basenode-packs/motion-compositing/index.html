Wipes driven by a 0 to 1 progress or the frame (linear, radial, clock, iris, by a gradient) whose edge covers exactly the share of the image the progress says, dissolves by noise or through a colour, a push; and the stylize effects of motion design: posterize, halftone dot screens, scanlines, a video tape look, seeded glitch blocks, a neon outline, a vignette and chromatic aberration.

The modules

A module is a .bn file of node groups. Once the pack is installed, a graph calls it with use, and Blender builds its groups.

ModuleWhat it gives
stylizeCompositor stylize effects of motion design: posterize in exact levels, halftone and colour halftone dot screens whose dots cover the luma and keep the alpha, scanlines, chroma offset, a video tape look (row jitter, chroma shift, noise, lines), seeded glitch blocks, a neon outline along a matte, a vignette and a lateral chromatic aberration
transitionsCompositor transitions driven by a 0..1 progress or the scene's frame: linear, radial, clock and iris wipes whose hard edge covers exactly progress of the image, at any angle and all around, a wipe by a gradient map, cross dissolve, dissolve by seeded noise cells, dip to a colour, push right, left, up or down

Free in Base Node, and what the pack adds

Free and open source, on the Base Node site: the language, the Blender add-on, the formatter and parsers, and the free modules of the standard library. The pack's modules build on the free modules grade, merge, qc. The pack adds the modules above, their showcase files and this tutorial.
