Cloners that instance a geometry along a line, on a circle, on a 2D or 3D grid, along a curve or on the points, faces and surface of a mesh, picking among a collection in turn or at random; effectors that move, turn and scale the clones at random, by a constant, step by step, by a noise, a sine or the time, and late or springy over the frames; falloffs that weigh them by a sphere, a box, an axis or a sector, inverted or combined. Each piped into the next.

The modules

A module is a .bn file of node groups. Once the pack is installed, a graph calls it with use, and Blender builds its groups.

ModuleWhat it gives
clonersCloners of a motion designer's toolset: a geometry instanced along a line, on a circle, on a 2D or 3D grid, along a curve, or on the points, faces or surface of a mesh, whole or picking among its instances in turn or at random by a seed; each clone stores its index and a factor from 0 to 1
effectorsEffectors that move, turn and scale the clones: at random by a seed, by a constant, by the clone's factor (step), by a noise in space and time, by a sine or the time, and late or springy behind where they go by a simulation over the frames; each weighed by a falloff
falloffsFalloffs that weigh an effector by where a clone is: in a sphere, a box, along an axis or in a sector around one, softened by a smoothstep, inverted or combined (maximum, minimum, multiply, add, subtract)

Free in Base Node, and what the pack adds

Free and open source, on the Base Node site: the language, the Blender add-on, the formatter and parsers, and the free modules of the standard library. The pack adds the modules above, their showcase files and this tutorial.
