Particles born on a point, a line, a circle or the outline of any shape or text, with a speed and a spread, gravity, drag and a flowing turbulence, a lifetime, seeded, drawn as sprites or dots that fade and grow over their life; a text revealed by character, word or line, forward, backward or in a random order, typed on, its letters offset, turned, scaled and faded about their centre and laid along a path; echoes of a moving element over the frames before, fading and tinted, and motion trails that thin to their tail. By simulation zones where the frames before matter.

The modules

A module is a .bn file of node groups. Once the pack is installed, a graph calls it with use, and Blender builds its groups.

ModuleWhat it gives
echoesTime echoes, after After Effects' Echo and Fusion's Trails: copies of a moving element as it was a few frames before, fading by a decay and tinted toward a colour, and motion trails, curves through where each point went over the last frames, thinning to their tail. In Geometry Nodes by a simulation zone that keeps the frames before: the compositor sees the current frame alone and has no memory of the others
particles2d2D particles in Geometry Nodes, after Fusion's and After Effects': emitters (a point, a line, a circle, any shape or text outline), a speed in a direction with a spread or out of the shape, gravity, drag and a flowing turbulence, a lifetime, all by a simulation zone and seeded; drawn as sprites or dots that fade and grow over their life. In Geometry Nodes because particles need a state kept from one frame to the next, which only a simulation zone gives: the compositor has none
text_animateText animation, after After Effects' text animators and range selectors: a text as one instance per character, revealed by character, word or line, forward, backward or in a random order by a seed, typed on, its characters offset, turned, scaled and faded by that reveal about their own centre, and laid along a path. In Geometry Nodes because only String to Curves makes letters, one instance each: the compositor has no text node

Free in Base Node, and what the pack adds

Free and open source, on the Base Node site: the language, the Blender add-on, the formatter and parsers, and the free modules of the standard library. The pack adds the modules above, their showcase files and this tutorial.
