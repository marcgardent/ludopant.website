Terrains on a grid: layered noise, terraces, thermal and hydraulic erosion that keep the ground and store their water, flow, wetness and sediment maps, and a slope mask. Voronoi fracture of a closed mesh into convex pieces, one island each, the cut faces marked, with gap, shrink and explode. Trees grown as a bracketed L-system rewrites them: branches as curves with their radius, tubes, and leaves at the tips.

The modules

A module is a .bn file of node groups. Once the pack is installed, a graph calls it with use, and Blender builds its groups.

ModuleWhat it gives
fractureVoronoi fracture of a closed mesh: points scattered inside it, each cut out as its convex cell by one Manifold Boolean of the half-spaces halfway to the other points, one island a piece with fracture_piece and fracture_inside on the cut faces, a gap between the cells, each piece shrunk about its centre, exploded from a centre
lsystemBranching grown as a bracketed L-system rewrites its tips (F -> F [&F] [&F] …) in a Repeat zone: side branches tilted by an angle and spun as phyllotaxis turns, a leader, length decay, jitter by seed, tropism; each segment a curve whose radius thins at each level, tubes of that radius, leaves at the last tips with their rotation
terrainTerrains on a grid of square cells: a heightfield of layered noise (fractal, ridged, warped) from 0 to its height, terraces exact to their levels, thermal erosion past a talus angle and hydraulic erosion through eight pipes per cell (rain, flow, sediment carried and laid down) that keep the ground, its maps stored on the points (water, sediment, wetness, flow, change of height), and a slope mask

Free in Base Node, and what the pack adds

Free and open source, on the Base Node site: the language, the Blender add-on, the formatter and parsers, and the free modules of the standard library. The pack adds the modules above, their showcase files and this tutorial.
