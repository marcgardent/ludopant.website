The atoms of a 2D texture graph: noises, cells and patterns that tile, shapes and a seeded tile sampler, then the filters that read a pixel's neighbours and wrap at the borders (blurs, warps, slope blur, edge detection, histogram scan and levels), and the maps a material needs from a height: a normal map in OpenGL or DirectX, curvature and ambient occlusion.

The modules

A module is a .bn file of node groups. Once the pack is installed, a graph calls it with use, and Blender builds its groups.

ModuleWhat it gives
texture_atomsCompositor generators of 2D textures that tile: Perlin noise and ridges read on a 4D torus so that they wrap, Voronoi cells of a grid that wraps, white noise, stripes and checker of whole periods, shapes (disc, square, polygon, star) with flat, cone and dome profiles, and a tile sampler scattering a shape over a grid with seeded random offset, size, rotation and luminance per tile and a random grey per tile
texture_filtersCompositor filters of 2D textures that wrap at the borders so a tiling texture still tiles: shifts and half offset, gaussian and directional blur, warp by the gradient of a map, directional warp, slope blur, Sobel edge detection, histogram scan, histogram range, levels and auto levels, height to tangent-space normal (OpenGL or DirectX), curvature from a normal map, ambient occlusion approximated from heights

Free in Base Node, and what the pack adds

Free and open source, on the Base Node site: the language, the Blender add-on, the formatter and parsers, and the free modules of the standard library. The pack's modules build on the free modules sdf2d, stmap. The pack adds the modules above, their showcase files and this tutorial.

CapabilityBlender's own nodesWith the pack
