The modules

A module is a .bn file of node groups. Once the pack is installed, a graph calls it with use, and Blender builds its groups.

ModuleWhat it gives
grainCompositor film grain after Nuke's Grain and Regrain: grain of its own size, irregularity and intensity per channel from noise textures on the pixels, seeded and animated, less of it in the blacks by a luma response, the Kodak stocks of Nuke's presets (5248, 5279, FX214, GT5274, 5217, 5218), regrain by the high pass of a grain plate, a simple degrain by bilateral blur
keyingCompositor keying tools after Nuke: IBK gizmo screen subtraction and IBK colour clean plate by push-pull, despill by average, red or blue limit or both with its spill map and luminance restore, matte clean, soft erode, choke, edge matte, core and edge merge, edge extend
lightCompositor light effects after Nuke: Glow of blurs of doubling size over a tolerance, LightWrap of the background on the foreground's edges by plus or screen, glow of exponential falloff, anamorphic streak of the highlights, glow around a matte's edges
passesCompositor tools on Cycles render passes: beauty rebuilt from its light passes, position, box, normal and depth mattes, depth fog, relight
vr360Compositor 360° projections after Nuke's SphericalTransform and Cara VR: a camera view of a latlong and back, latlong rotation by yaw, pitch and roll, 3 × 2 cubemap and back, equidistant fisheye and back, little planet, each as its STMap and its image

Free in Base Node, and what the pack adds

Free and open source, on the Base Node site: the language, the Blender add-on, the formatter and parsers, and the free modules of the standard library. The pack's modules build on the free modules grade, merge, stmap. The pack adds the modules above, their showcase files and this tutorial.
