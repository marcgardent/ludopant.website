Mesh maps computed live and stored as attributes (curvature, ambient occlusion, thickness, position, islands, sky), the generators that turn them into masks (edge wear, metal edge wear, dirt, dust, grunge, dripping rust) and the wear only a mesh can carry (chipped edges, dents, rivets, stitches, streaks and drips).

The modules

A module is a .bn file of node groups. Once the pack is installed, a graph calls it with use, and Blender builds its groups.

ModuleWhat it gives
generatorsThe generators of a texture painter's smart materials, as shader groups that turn the mesh maps into masks: levels (Histogram Scan), grunge, curvature, occlusion, edge wear, metal edge wear, dirt, dust, dripping rust, position, 3D distance, light
mesh_effectsWear a texture painter can only paint, made in the mesh by Geometry Nodes: chips knocked off the edges, dents, rivets and stitches along the seams, rust and dirt streaked down the walls from their tops and the rivets as a stain, paint or oil run down them in the material under them, wearing what it wears, dust settled in the corners; each stores where it acted, and a shader group reads it
mesh_mapsMesh maps computed live by Geometry Nodes and stored as attributes, as a texture painter bakes them: curvature, ambient occlusion, thickness, position, islands, and the sky a point sees above it; and the shader group that reads them

Free in Base Node, and what the pack adds

Free and open source, on the Base Node site: the language, the Blender add-on, the formatter and parsers, and the free modules of the standard library. The pack adds the modules above, their showcase files and this tutorial.
